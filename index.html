When a mighty baobab falls, the whole village feels the earth tremble; the birds mourn their shelter, the weary remember its shade, and generations grieve the loss of its towering presence. Yet its seeds remain, carrying the promise of new roots, renewed shade, and life yet to come. So it is with her passing: though her branches have fallen, the wisdom she planted, the lives she touched, and the legacy she leaves behind will continue to grow in all of us.
